🔄 19 October 2026 — Go-Live Stock Take

This is the controlled reset point for the new live system. It does not delete previous history. You will scan the cylinders physically at your company and then scan the cylinders physically held by each customer.

🔄 GO-LIVE STOCK TAKE — 19 OCTOBER 2026

Official reset date: 19 October 2026

Use this once, after physically checking the cylinders. Previous movements remain in the history. The reset establishes the new opening position for stock and Rental.

1. 📦 Company Stock

Scan every cylinder physically in your warehouse on 19 October.

2. 👥 Customer Opening Stock

Select a customer, then scan every cylinder physically held by that customer on 19 October.

3. 🧮 Unbarcoded Older Stock

For older cylinders that are physically in your company but still have no barcode. Enter the quantity by cylinder type. These are kept separate from barcode stock.

4. Review & Confirm

Before confirming, physically compare the totals with your stock count and customer count. This action is permanent for the 19 October reset date.
